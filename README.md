### 🎨 Indie & Experimental

#### [Itch.io](https://itch.io/games/platform-web)
*   **Best For:** Discovering experimental, artistic, and horror indie games.

*   **Cons:** Quality varies wildly (open platform).

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Are these games safe to play?**
A: Yes, all the platforms listed above (especially the curated ones like 61673 and Poki) scan their games for malware. However, always be cautious about clicking on ads on the "Massive Library" sites.

**Q: Do I need to download anything?**
A: No! That's the beauty of HTML5 games. They run directly in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari) without any plugins or downloads.

**Q: Do I need an Ad-Blocker?**
A: For sites like **61673 Games**, you don't need one because there are no ads! For other sites like CrazyGames or Y8, an ad-blocker might improve your experience, but some sites may ask you to turn it off.

**Q: What happened to Flash games?**
A: Adobe Flash was discontinued in 2020. Most modern sites use HTML5 technologies (Canvas, WebGL, WebAssembly) or emulators (like Ruffle) to run legacy games.
